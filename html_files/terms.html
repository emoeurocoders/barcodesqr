2. Static and Dynamic QR Codes

BarcodesQR may provide both static and dynamic QR codes.

Static QR Codes

A static QR code directly contains the destination or information encoded in the QR code itself.

Once downloaded, a properly created static QR code generally does not require an active BarcodesQR subscription to continue functioning.

However, we cannot guarantee that a static QR code will remain useful if the destination URL, telephone number, website, third-party service, or other information encoded in the QR code later changes or becomes unavailable.

Dynamic QR Codes

Dynamic QR codes use BarcodesQR infrastructure to redirect a person scanning the QR code to the destination you have configured.

Dynamic QR codes may allow you to change the destination after the QR code has been printed and may provide analytics and other features.

Because dynamic QR codes depend on BarcodesQR infrastructure and an active account, dynamic QR codes and related hosted services may become inactive or stop redirecting if your applicable subscription expires, is cancelled, is suspended, or is otherwise no longer active.

You are responsible for determining whether a static or dynamic QR code is appropriate for your particular use.
